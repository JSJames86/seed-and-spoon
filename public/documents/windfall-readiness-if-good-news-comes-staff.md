# Seed & Spoon — For Our Team & Volunteers

## If Good News Comes

*What a big, unexpected gift would — and wouldn't — change. So you hear it from us, plainly.*

Someday Seed & Spoon may receive a large, unrestricted gift. If that happens, here is exactly what to expect. No spin.

-----

## What will not change

**The mission.** Every dollar still answers one question: does this get a meal to a hungry kid? More money means we do the mission more completely — not that we chase new, shinier things.

**How carefully we work.** We still trace every claim and every dollar. We spend on evidence, not on excitement.

**Who we are.** We build our own tools, we stay lean on purpose, and we keep our feet in the community. A full bank account doesn't turn us into a different organization.

**You.** The people who showed up when it was hard are the heart of this. A windfall is meant to support the team, not replace its spirit.

-----

## What we'd do first

**Pause.** A real week to think before spending a cent.

**Protect the basics.** Compliance, controls, and fair support for the people doing the work — before anything glamorous.

**Tell you first.** Our team and our existing donors hear it from us before any public announcement.

**Stay focused.** A few things done exceptionally well, instead of many things done halfway.

-----

## The promise

A game-changing grant changes what we can do. It never changes why we do it, or how carefully. If you ever hear a rumor that worries you, bring it straight to us — we'd rather tell you the truth than let silence fill in the blanks.
