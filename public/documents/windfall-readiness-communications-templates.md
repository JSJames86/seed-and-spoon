# Seed & Spoon — Confidential Templates

## Pre-Committed Communications

*Windfall announcement drafts — ready before we ever need them.*

Fill the brackets when the moment comes. These exist so we never compose our identity under deadline.

Sequence matters: send the internal message first, then existing donors, then partners, then any public statement. Never let the public learn before our own team does.

-----

## A — Internal Message — Staff, Volunteers & Board

**Channel:** Email + in-person / all-hands where possible.
**Timing:** Same day the gift is confirmed, before any external word.

**Subject:** Some big news — and what it means for us

> Team,
>
> Seed & Spoon has received a gift of [amount / "a significant, unrestricted gift"] from [funder, or "a donor who asked to stay private"]. It is unrestricted, which means we decide how to use it — and that is exactly why I want you to hear the plan from me first.
>
> Here is what will not change: our mission, our care with every dollar, and the way we work. Every dollar still answers one question — does this get a meal to a hungry kid? This gift lets us do the mission more completely; it does not turn us into a different organization.
>
> Here is what happens next: we are taking a deliberate pause before spending anything. We will fund the unglamorous essentials first — compliance, controls, and fair support for the people doing the work. Then we will choose a small number of things to do exceptionally well. You will be part of that conversation, not surprised by it.
>
> If you hear questions or rumors out in the community, point people to me. Thank you for building this with me — this news is yours as much as mine.
>
> [Name] — Founder & Executive Director, Seed & Spoon

## B — External Message — Tiered

### Tier 1 — Existing Donors

**Subject:** A milestone — and why your support still matters

> Dear [Name],
>
> I want you to be among the first to know: Seed & Spoon has received [a significant unrestricted gift / a gift of amount] from [funder]. It is a milestone, and I am sharing it with you directly because your support helped make us the kind of organization a gift like this finds.
>
> A gift like this is runway, not a finish line. It does not replace the steady support of donors like you — it is built on the foundation you helped lay, and your continued partnership is exactly what turns a one-time gift into lasting impact. Nothing about our commitment to you, or to the families we serve, changes.
>
> I would be glad to tell you more about how we are thinking about it. Thank you — truly.
>
> [Name], Founder & Executive Director

### Tier 2 — Partners & Allies

**Audience:** Churches, kitchens (e.g. Garden State Kitchen), community organizations, fiscal-sponsor and clinic partners.

**Subject:** News from Seed & Spoon — and what it means for our work together

> Hi [Name],
>
> Some good news to share: Seed & Spoon has received [a significant unrestricted gift / a gift of amount]. I wanted you to hear it from us, because our work together is part of what this makes possible.
>
> This strengthens our partnership — it doesn't change the terms of it. We remain committed to [shared program / commitment], and if anything, this gives us more capacity to show up well. I'd welcome a conversation about where added resources could deepen what we're already building together.
>
> Grateful for the partnership,
> [Name], Seed & Spoon

### Tier 3 — Public / Press / General Audience

**Use:** Website, social, or press — only after internal, donor, and partner messages have gone out.

**Subject / headline:** Seed & Spoon receives major gift to expand food-security work in Newark

> Seed & Spoon, a Newark-based food-security nonprofit, has received [a significant unrestricted gift / a gift of amount] from [funder]. The organization will direct the gift toward [high-level intention — e.g. strengthening its weekend meal program and community food-access tools], with a deliberate focus on long-term sustainability rather than short-term expansion.
>
> "[Short, human quote from founder — grounded in mission, not self-congratulation. Example: This gift lets us do the work more completely for the families we serve, and we intend to steward every dollar with the same care that got us here.]"
>
> [Optional: one line on how to support or get involved — seedandspoon.org.]
